Privacy Policy for DevAI Study

Last Updated: November 2026

DevAI Study ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our application within the Pi Network Ecosystem and PiNet.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in using Pi Authentication, we only access your public Pi Username and unique user identifier (UID) as authorized by you. We do not collect passwords, passphrases, or sensitive personal records.

2. How We Use Information

Your Pi Username is used solely to personalize your study sessions and track your learning progress locally on your device. AI queries are processed via Google Gemini API to generate educational responses and are not sold to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your study history and preferences are stored locally in your browser's LocalStorage. You can clear this data at any time from your browser settings.

4. Contact

For questions regarding this policy, contact the developer via the Pi Ecosystem.
